### Steadier Panama PQ Training and Encoding

**Description**

With the Panama provider on JDK 22, PQ training and encoding could run 3-15x slower in some JVMs, because the
small-vector distance loops were sometimes compiled in a way that allocated on every distance. The Panama provider
now runs these loops in their own methods, as the native provider does. PQ training and encoding are also 7-10%
faster with Panama on JDK 22 and 25.

**How to Enable**

No configuration is required.
