### DOT_PRODUCT Resumes Keep Negative Scores and the Search Threshold

**Description**

DOT_PRODUCT scores are `(1 + dot) / 2`, negative for vectors that are not unit length when the dot product is below -1. `GraphSearcher.resume(additionalK, rerankK)`, the rerank floor of `GraphSearcher.search(scoreProvider, topK, threshold, acceptOrds)`, the static `GraphSearcher.search(queryVector, topK, rerankK, ...)` and the searches in `OnDiskGraphIndexCompactor` used 0 and dropped such nodes: resumes never returned them, searches with a reranker returned one result, and compaction could leave a node without edges to its best matches. They now use negative infinity, as the graph builder and the hierarchy descent already did. `resume(additionalK, rerankK)` also now applies the threshold of the search it continues. COSINE and EUCLIDEAN top-k searches are unaffected.

**How to Enable**

No configuration is required.
