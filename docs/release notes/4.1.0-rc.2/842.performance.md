### Faster PQ Query Tables for Subvectors Two or Three SIMD Vectors Wide

**Description**

With the Panama provider, a dot product over a slice two or three SIMD vectors wide, such as a PQ subvector, called a
method too large for the JIT to inline. Such slices now take a small path the JIT inlines, with the same results up to
float rounding. This extends [#816](https://github.com/datastax/jvector/pull/816), which covered slices exactly one
vector wide, to 8- or 12-float subvectors on 128-bit SIMD, 16 or 24 on AVX2 and 32 or 48 on AVX-512. `DOT_PRODUCT` and
`COSINE` tables gain; `EUCLIDEAN` tables already used a routine small enough to inline and are unchanged, as are longer
vectors. The native provider builds each table in one native call and is not affected.

**How to Enable**

No configuration is required.

**Notes**

Building the PQ table for a 1024-dimension query took 13-50% less time with 32-float subvectors at 512-bit width on Ice
Lake (8 cores) and GNR (48 cores), JDK 22 and 25, and 62% less with 16-float subvectors at 256-bit width. End to end on
a 1M-vector dataset, single-thread PQ search ran 19% faster at 512-bit width and 3.1 times as fast at 256-bit width.
Subvectors with a partial last vector of four or more floats on AVX2 or AVX-512 (for example 12 floats on AVX2 or 24 on
AVX-512) now finish on narrower vectors instead of one float at a time: their tables took 18-40% less time. On Graviton4
(ARM), PQ search with 7- and 8-float subvectors ran 26-29% faster with 8 search cores. Recall was unchanged.
