### Faster ASH Scoring at 3 and 5 to 9 Bits with Panama

**Description**

With the Panama provider, 3 and 5 to 9-bit ASH codes are now decoded in SIMD registers instead of scalar
Java, in single-vector scoring, fused upper layers and fused base-layer neighborhoods. On JDK 22, Ice Lake,
these paths are 8 to 25 times faster. The 2 and 4-bit Panama kernel on JDK 22 also decodes in registers
instead of gathering query elements, about 3.7 times faster for single vectors. Scores are unchanged
within each kernel's existing tolerance, and every similarity (`DOT_PRODUCT`, `COSINE`, `EUCLIDEAN`) is
now tested at every ASH setting.

**How to Enable**

No configuration is required.
